# Profile: source material for tailored resumes

Put Vikas's own material here. Tailored resumes are built only from facts in this folder.

- `about.md`: summary, years of experience, current role, notice period, location, links (LinkedIn, GitHub, portfolio)
- `experience.md`: each job with dates, team, what you built, and impact (numbers where real)
- `skills.md`: languages, frameworks, databases, cloud, tools, with strength level
- `projects.md`: personal or work projects with stack, what you did, and links
- `base-resumes/`: your existing resumes (PDF or DOCX)
